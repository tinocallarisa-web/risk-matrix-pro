WHAT IT DOES
Likelihood x impact risk matrix, 3x3 to 10x10 (auto-sized), one marker per risk,
cells coloured by score band.

TEST - PRO (node build-test.js: isPro true, GUID _test)
1. Risk=RiskID, Category, Likelihood, Impact: 5x5 matrix, legend, markers.
2. Hover a marker: tooltip. Click: other visuals filter. Ctrl+click adds. Click a
   cell or legend entry: selects its risks. Click empty space: clears.
3. Add Residual and Target likelihood/impact. Hover a risk: path inherent ->
   residual (solid) -> target (dashed). Red dashed ring = above target.
4. Risk appetite on, Custom thresholds on, Cell detail panel on (click a cell),
   Shape by category on, Marker color fx rule: all apply. No watermark.
5. Tab into the matrix, arrows move, Enter selects, Esc clears, Shift+F10 menu.

GETTING STARTED
1. Drop the visual on the canvas.
2. Drag the risk ID into Risk. Short IDs (R01, R02…) read best inside the markers.
3. Drag the likelihood into Likelihood and the impact into Impact.
4. Optional: a Category (type, owner, status) colours the markers and builds the legend.
5. Optional (Pro): Residual and Target likelihood and impact show where each risk goes.

FIELD WELLS
• Risk ............................... one marker per value
• Category ........................... colours, legend, and shapes in Pro
• Likelihood / Impact ................ position on the grid
• Residual likelihood / impact (Pro) . position after controls — add both
• Target likelihood / impact (Pro) ... position to reach — add both

TIPS
• One row per risk. If Power BI shows "Sum of Likelihood", set the field to
  Don't summarize, Maximum or Average.
• Leave Grid size on Auto. It matches the highest level in your data.
• Name the levels: Axes → level labels, e.g. Rare, Unlikely, Possible, Likely,
  Almost certain.
• Crowded matrix? Switch Movement arrows to "Selected and hovered": a path then
  appears only when you point at a risk, focus it or select it.
• Place markers at Residual to show today's exposure instead of the starting point.
• Use the red ring as the agenda: those risks are still above their target.
• Custom thresholds (Pro) set where Medium, High and Critical start; the appetite
  score draws the line above which action is required.
• Busy cell? "+n" counts the markers that do not fit. The cell detail panel (Pro)
  lists them all.
• Take the risk list to the meeting (Pro): Risk list → Show risk list, then Sort by
  Gap to target. Point at a row to find its marker.
• Pick a style (Pro) under Style template. Colours you set yourself stay; Classic
  brings back the 1.0 look.
• Small tile? Text shrinks down to 60 % and never under 8 px. Raise
  Accessibility → Minimum text size if your audience needs larger text.
• Format on the canvas: in format mode, click a cell, a marker, an axis, the legend
  or the risk list to format it in place.
• Setup guide: while editing, the corner card ticks off the optional fields.
  Close it with ✕; bring it back with Style template → Setup guide.
• A second click on the same marker or cell clears the selection.
• While editing, the yellow strip reports risks that could not be plotted or
  values outside the scale.
• Cross-filtering a table needs the relationship direction set to Both in Model view.

YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION — PRODUCT VIDEO
==========================================

A risk register in a table does not tell you where to look. A risk matrix does: likelihood by impact, in score bands, every risk in its cell. Risk Matrix Pro also shows where each risk is going — inherent, residual after controls, and the target you still have to reach — and ranks them in a list beside the matrix, so the ones that still need work come first.

──────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT IT DOES
──────────────────────────────────────────
✅ Every risk on a 3x3 to 10x10 grid, or sized automatically from your data
✅ Levels (1–5), probabilities (0–1) or percentages (0–100) as input
✅ Inherent → residual → target, with movement arrows
✅ A ranked risk list beside the matrix: point at a row and its marker lights up
✅ A dashed red ring on every risk still above its target
✅ Your risk appetite drawn as a line across the matrix
✅ Report theme, Classic and the six TCViz collection styles, always reading green → red
✅ Click a cell or a risk to filter the report; click again to clear
✅ Text and markers that scale with the visual; keyboard, high contrast and a minimum text size
✅ The measure's number format, dynamic format strings included; twelve languages
✅ No network requests: everything is computed inside your report

A risk register in a table tells you what the risks are. A risk matrix tells you where they are — and
Risk Matrix Pro also tells you where they are going.

Every risk becomes a marker on a likelihood x impact grid, coloured by score band. Point at a risk and
its whole path appears: inherent position, residual position after controls, and the target you still
have to reach. Risks that have not reached their target carry a red ring, so the ones that need
attention stand out before anyone reads a number.

Built for risk registers in audit, compliance, cybersecurity, safety, project and enterprise risk
management.

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH IT

• Place every risk on a 3x3 to 10x10 grid — or let the grid size itself from your data
• Use levels (1–5), probabilities (0–1) or percentages (0–100) as input
• See which risks have not reached their target at a glance
• Show the effect of controls: inherent → residual → target, one risk at a time
• Draw your risk appetite as a line across the matrix
• Click a cell to list every risk inside it
• Cross-filter the rest of the report by risk, cell or category

PRIVACY

The visual makes no network requests of any kind. No analytics, no telemetry, no external scripts.
Its privileges array is empty, so Power BI blocks outbound connections at the platform level. Your
data never leaves the report.

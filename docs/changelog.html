1.1.0.0 2026-11-23

Added

  • Risk list (Pro). Format → Risk list → Show risk list: the risks ranked beside the matrix by inherent score, residual score or gap to target, each with its band and its path (inherent → residual → target) in the band colours, and a red dot when it is still above target. Point at a row to light up its marker; click a row to select the risk. Width adjustable (20–50 %).
  • The six collection styles (Pro). Style template → Boardroom, Ledger, Editorial, Signal, Aurora or Terrace, the same styles as the rest of the TCViz visuals. Each style brings its own version of the four risk colours: they still read green → red, in the tone of the style. Category colours follow the style palette. Without a licence a style is drawn while you edit, under the "Pro preview" watermark; reading view shows Report theme.
  • Report theme and Classic templates (free). Report theme (the default) takes text and axis colours from the report theme; Classic is the look of 1.0.
  • Text that scales with the visual. Below report size (760 × 480 px) axes, cell scores, legend, labels, the detail panel and the markers shrink together, down to 60 % and never under 8 px. No label is removed to make room.
  • Accessibility card. High contrast Automatic (Windows) or On, focus colour and width for keyboard navigation, minimum text size.
  • Format on the canvas (on-object formatting). In format mode, click the cells, the markers, an axis, the legend or the risk list to format them where they are. A click in format mode never filters the page.
  • Setup guide. While you edit, a card in the corner lists the optional fields (Category, Residual, Target, Tooltips) with a tick when each is in place, and the styles. Close it with ✕; turn it back on under Style template. It is hidden on tiles smaller than 420 × 280 px.
  • 12 languages. English, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), French, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified) and Korean: the format pane, the field wells and every text drawn by the visual.
  • Dynamic format strings are declared: tooltips and the detail panel use the format of each row, in the report language.

Changed

  • Movement shown by default. With residual or target fields, markers now sit at the inherent position and every risk's path is drawn (Movement arrows → All risks). Before, markers sat at the residual position and the path appeared only for the hovered or selected risk. *Reports that never changed these settings will show the paths.*
  • One marker size. All markers share the size that fits the busiest cell, so a lone risk is no longer drawn larger than its neighbours when the visual shrinks.
  • Marker labels switch between black and white to contrast with their marker, unless you set a label colour.
  • Selection. Clicking the same cell or marker again clears the selection; the selected cell gets a border and the other cells dim.
  • Interactions follow the report setting read from the host capabilities.
  • "Pro preview" watermark is the same as in the rest of the collection.

1.0.1.0 2026-09-21

Changed

  • Localization connected (English and Spanish), brand icon and resubmission fixes.

1.0.0.0 2026-09-16

First public release.

Added

  • Likelihood × impact matrix from 3 × 3 to 10 × 10, sized automatically from the data, with levels, fractions or percentages as input.
  • Automatic score bands (Low, Medium, High, Critical) with configurable colours.
  • One marker per risk, fitted to busy cells, with a +n badge when a cell holds more than fit.
  • Category colours, legend (click to select a category) and per-category colour pickers.
  • Axis titles and level labels, with fonts and colours for axes, legend and cell text.
  • Tooltips and report page tooltips, including up to 5 extra measures with their model format.
  • Selection, multi-selection, cross-filtering, highlighting and bookmarks.
  • Keyboard navigation (arrow keys, Home / End, Page Up / Page Down, Enter, Escape, Shift + F10), screen reader labels and high contrast support.
  • Data warnings for risks without likelihood or impact, values outside the scale, and residual or target fields added without their pair.
  • Pro: residual and target positions with movement arrows, shown for the selected or hovered risk by default; flag for risks above their target; custom score thresholds; risk appetite line; conditional marker colours; shapes by category; cell detail panel.
  • Pro preview: without a licence, Pro capabilities used while editing render under a "Pro preview" watermark, with Power BI's licence notification. Reading view shows the free result.

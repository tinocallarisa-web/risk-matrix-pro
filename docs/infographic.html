  • Shows movement and targets, not only today's position

Tips

  • Use short IDs (R01, R02) in Risk — they fit inside markers
  • One row per risk: avoid "Sum of Likelihood"
  • Name the levels: Rare … Almost certain
  • Place markers at Residual to show today's exposure
  • Shapes by category read in black and white
  • Cross-filter to a table needs direction Both

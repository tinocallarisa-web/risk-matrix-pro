Risk Matrix Pro — a Power BI custom visual by TCViz · Version 1.1.0.0 · Last updated: 23 November 2026

Risk Matrix Pro places every risk of a register on a likelihood × impact grid, coloured by score band. Pro adds where each risk is going: its residual position after controls, its target, how far it still is from that target, a ranked risk list beside the matrix, your risk appetite drawn across the matrix and the six collection styles.

What's new in 1.1

  • Risk list Pro — the risks ranked beside the matrix by inherent score, residual score or gap to target, each with its path in the band colours. See Risk list below.
  • Six collection styles Pro — Boardroom, Ledger, Editorial, Signal, Aurora and Terrace, each with its own version of the four band colours.
  • Report theme and Classic templates (free) — Report theme, the new default, takes text and axis colours from the report theme; Classic is the look of 1.0.
  • Movement shown by default — with residual or target fields, markers now sit at the inherent position and every risk's path is drawn. Reports that never changed these settings will show the paths.
  • Text that scales with the visual, an Accessibility card, format on the canvas, a setup guide and 12 languages.
  • One marker size for every cell, marker labels that switch between black and white, and a second click on the same cell or marker clears the selection.

Quick start

  1. Drop the visual on the canvas.
  2. Drag the risk ID or name into Risk. Each row becomes one marker.
  3. Drag the likelihood into Likelihood and the impact into Impact. The matrix appears, sized from your data.
  4. Optional: add a Category (type, owner, status) to colour the markers and build the legend.
  5. Optional Pro: add Residual and Target likelihood and impact to show movement and targets.

Input values and grid size

Likelihood and impact can arrive in any of these forms — the visual detects which one with Grid → Input values → Auto, or you can set it:

Input valuesExamplePlaced at
Levels3Level 3
Fraction (0 to 1)0.45 on a 5 × 5 gridLevel 3
Percent (0 to 100)45 on a 5 × 5 gridLevel 3

Grid size → Auto (from data) uses the highest level in your data, from 3 × 3 to 10 × 10. With fractions or percentages there is no natural maximum, so it uses 5 × 5. Choose a fixed size to override it.

How scores and bands work

A cell's score is likelihood × impact. The cell colour comes from its band:

BandAutomatic ruleOn a 5 × 5 grid
Lowup to 16% of the maximum score1 – 4
Mediumup to 36%5 – 9
Highup to 64%10 – 16
Criticalabove 64%20 – 25

With Custom thresholds Pro you set the score where Medium, High and Critical start, to match your own methodology.

Inherent, residual and target Pro

  • Inherent — the risk before controls (Likelihood and Impact).
  • Residual — the risk with today's controls.
  • Target — where you aim to be once planned actions are done.

By default (since 1.1) markers sit at the inherent position and every risk's path is drawn (Movement arrows → All risks): a solid arrow for what has been achieved, a dashed arrow for what remains, a small circle for the other positions and a diamond for the target. On a crowded matrix, set Movement arrows → Selected and hovered risks: a path then appears only when you point at a risk, focus it with the keyboard or select it. Place markers at moves the markers to the residual or target position.

A risk whose current score (residual, or inherent if there is none) is still above its target score carries a dashed red ring. The tooltip and the cell detail panel say whether the target is reached.

Risk list Pro

Turn on Risk list → Show risk list to rank the risks in a column beside the matrix — the list a committee takes to the meeting.

  • Each row shows the risk name and its path as score chips coloured by band: inherent → residual → target. A red dot marks a risk still above its target (when Flag risks above target is on).
  • Sort by: Inherent score (default), Residual score (the current score: residual, or inherent if there is none) or Gap to target (how far the current score is above the target; risks without a target go last).
  • Width (%): 20 to 50 % of the visual (34 % by default).
  • Point at a row, or focus it with Tab, to light up its marker; click a row to select the risk (Ctrl + click adds).

Paid settings and field wells are marked (Pro) in Power BI. Without a licence:

  • While you edit a report, Pro capabilities you use are shown working under a Pro preview watermark, and Power BI shows how to get a licence.
  • In reading view and published reports, the free result is shown: inherent positions, automatic bands, Report theme instead of a collection style, no appetite line, no movement, no risk list, no detail panel. No watermark.

Interaction

  • Click a marker — selects the risk and filters the rest of the page. Click it again to clear the selection.
  • Ctrl / Cmd + click — adds risks to the selection.
  • Click a cell — selects every risk in it (and opens the detail panel in Pro). The selected cell gets a border and the other cells dim; click the same cell again to clear.
  • Click a row of the risk list Pro — selects that risk.
  • Click a legend entry — selects every risk in that category.
  • Click empty space — clears the selection.
  • Right click — the standard Power BI menu (Include, Exclude, Drill through).
  • Filters from other visuals dim the risks that do not match instead of hiding them.
  • Interactions follow the report setting; in format mode a click never filters the page.

Accessibility

  • Keyboard — Tab enters the matrix; arrow keys move to the nearest risk in that direction; Home / End jump to the first and last; Page Up / Page Down move ten at a time; Enter or Space selects (Ctrl adds); Escape clears; Shift + F10 opens the context menu. The tooltip also appears on focus.
  • Screen readers — each risk announces its name, category, likelihood, impact, score, band and, in Pro, residual score and whether the target is reached.
  • High contrast — system colours only; the band is encoded as border thickness instead of fill, and the appetite line becomes dashed. Accessibility → High contrast follows Windows (Automatic) or can be forced On.
  • Focus ring — colour and width under Accessibility → Focus color / Focus width.
  • Minimum text size — no text is drawn smaller than this, even when the visual shrinks.

Text that scales with the visual

At report size (760 × 480 px or larger) text and markers use the sizes you set. Below that, axes, cell scores, legend, labels, the detail panel, the risk list and the markers shrink together, down to 60 % and never under 8 px (or the Minimum text size, if higher). No label is removed to make room.

Format on the canvas

In format mode, click the cells, the markers, an axis, the legend or the risk list to format them where they are (on-object formatting). A click in format mode never filters the page.

Setup guide

While you edit, a card in the corner lists the optional fields — Category, Residual likelihood + impact, Target likelihood + impact, Tooltips — with a tick when each is in place, and the style templates. Close it with ✕; turn it back on with Style template → Setup guide. It never shows in reading view or format mode, and it is hidden on tiles smaller than 420 × 280 px.

Languages

The format pane, the field wells and every text drawn by the visual follow the report language: English, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), French, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified) and Korean. Tooltips and the detail panel use the format string of each row (dynamic format strings), in the report language.

Data warnings

A yellow strip at the bottom-left tells you when:

  • risks have no likelihood or impact and cannot be plotted;
  • values fall outside the scale (a 7 on a 5 × 5 grid, 1.4 as a fraction) and were placed at the edge;
  • a residual or target field was added without its pair.

By default it shows only while editing, because it is meant for the report author.

Troubleshooting

All risks sit in the bottom-left corner

The grid is bigger than your data. Set Grid → Grid size → Auto (from data), or the size that matches your scale.

Every risk is in the same column or row

The input scale was detected differently from what you expect — for example probabilities stored as whole percentages. Set Grid → Input values explicitly.

The field shows "Sum of Likelihood" and values look too high

Power BI is adding the likelihood of several rows. Make sure each risk appears once in your table, or set the field's summarisation to Don't summarize, Maximum or Average.

No arrows appear

Arrows need both residual (or both target) fields and a Pro licence (or the Pro preview while editing). Check that Movement and target → Movement arrows is not set to Off.

Why do all paths show now?

Since 1.1 the default is Movement arrows → All risks with markers at the inherent position, so every risk's journey is visible without pointing at it. Reports that never changed these settings show the paths after the update. To get the 1.0 behaviour back, set Movement arrows → Selected and hovered risks and Place markers at → Residual position.

Can I keep the classic colours?

Yes. Set Style template → Template → Classic for the look of 1.0. Band colours you already picked under Score bands are kept with any template.

Where is the risk list?

Under Risk list → Show risk list (Pro). It is off by default. Without a licence it is shown only while you edit, under the Pro preview watermark.

The text looks smaller than I set it

Below report size (760 × 480 px) text shrinks with the visual, down to 60 % and never under 8 px. Make the visual larger, or raise Accessibility → Minimum text size.

I chose a style but reading view shows something else

The six collection styles are Pro. Without a licence they are previewed while you edit and reading view shows Report theme. High contrast also replaces the style.

Labels inside the markers are cut short

Labels are fitted to the marker. Use short IDs in Risk, increase Markers → Marker size, or raise Label max characters.

Clicking a risk does not filter another table

Check the relationship between the tables in Model view. Filtering from a fact table to a dimension table needs the cross-filter direction set to Both.

Pro features are not active after purchasing

The visual detects the licence when the report is opened or refreshed. Microsoft notes it can take up to an hour for a new licence to be recognised: refresh the browser (F5) or close and reopen Power BI Desktop. A licence is required per user. If it persists, email us with your AppSource order reference.

Limits and known behaviour

  • Up to 1,000 risks and 5 tooltip measures.
  • Grid from 3 × 3 to 10 × 10.
  • The risk list takes 20 to 50 % of the width and lists every loaded risk.
  • The setup guide is hidden on tiles smaller than 420 × 280 px.
  • When a cell holds more markers than fit at the minimum size, a +n badge counts the rest; the detail panel Pro lists them all.
  • Scores use levels (1 to grid size). Fractions and percentages are converted to levels first, so two risks at 0.41 and 0.59 on a 5 × 5 grid share a cell.
